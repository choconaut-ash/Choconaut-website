ABOUT THE MISSION

At Choco-naut™, we don't just make chocolate; we craft premium bean-to-bar artisan chocolate using traditional methods. Our journey begins on the lush, tropical estates of Idukki (Kerala) and Dakshina Kannada (Karnataka), India's finest cacao-growing regions.

Unlike mass-produced candy, we source single-origin Indian cacao beans known for their fruity, spicy, and woody flavor profiles. We work directly with Indian farmers to ensure ethical sourcing, sustainable farming, and premium quality fermentation, making us one of India's leading craft chocolate brands.

OUR BEAN TO BAR PROCESS

Traditional 4-Stage Artisan Chocolate Making Process

🔥

1. SLOW ROAST

We gently roast Indian cacao beans at low temperatures to unlock hidden aromatic profiles and preserve natural antioxidants.

🌪️

2. CRACK & WINNOW

The roasted beans are cracked and husks are removed using traditional air flow winnowing technique.

⚙️

3. STONE GRIND

We stone-grind cacao nibs with organic cane sugar in melangers for 72 hours to create smooth, premium chocolate.

🌡️

4. TEMPER & MOLD

The liquid chocolate is hand-tempered for that perfect glossy finish, crisp snap, and artisan quality.
