ABOUT THE MISSION

At Choco-naut™, we don't just make chocolate; we engineer an experience. Our journey begins on the lush, tropical estates of Idukki (Kerala) and Dakshina Kannada (Karnataka).

Unlike mass-produced candy, we source single-origin Indian Cacao beans known for their fruity, spicy, and woody notes. We work directly with farmers to ensure ethical sourcing and premium quality fermentation.

FLIGHT TRAJECTORY

Our 4-Stage Protocol from Bean to Bar

🔥

1. SLOW ROAST

We gently roast the beans at low temperatures to unlock the hidden aromatic profiles.

🌪️

2. CRACK & WINNOW

The beans are cracked and the shells (husk) are removed using air flow.

⚙️

3. STONE GRIND

We grind the nibs with organic sugar in stone melangers for 72 hours continuously.

🌡️

4. TEMPER & MOLD

The liquid chocolate is tempered to give it that perfect glossy finish and snap.
